HARVANA PROTOCOL & MULTI-CHAIN ECOSYSTEM
GLOBAL PRIVACY POLICY & DATA SUBJECT RIGHTS
Last Updated: October 2026 | Effective Worldwide

1. CORE PRIVACY PRINCIPLE: ZERO PERSONAL DATA MINING
Harvana is built on the cryptographic principle of data minimization and sovereign privacy. We do not sell, rent, monetize, or harvest personal data. There are no third-party tracking scripts, advertising SDKs, or data broker integrations within the Harvana application.

2. ON-CHAIN IMMUTABILITY & PUBLIC LEDGER TRANSPARENCY
Cryptographic transactions conducted on Harvana, Ethereum, Bitcoin, BNB Chain, Polygon, Arbitrum, Solana, and other supported distributed networks are recorded on public, decentralized ledgers. By initiating on-chain transfers, smart contract interactions, or savings circle deposits, you acknowledge that blockchain transactions are permanent, transparent, and mathematically immutable.

3. LOCAL ENCRYPTION OF KEYS & RECOVERY PHRASES
All cryptographic private keys, seed phrases, and security PINs are encrypted locally on your device hardware using Android KeyStore, hardware-backed Keystore, or AES-GCM-256 encryption. Private keys NEVER leave your physical device and are NEVER transmitted to any central server.

4. FIRESTORE & DISTRIBUTED CACHING
To provide high-performance price quotes, coin metadata, and P2P order matching without rate-limiting:
- Public market prices, token logos, and contract ABIs are cached globally in Firestore.
- P2P trade chats and encrypted payment slips are visible only to the buyer, seller, and certified dispute arbitrators.
- Optional profile information (username, bio, avatar) can be updated or cleared by you at any time.

5. GDPR, CCPA, & GLOBAL PRIVACY COMPLIANCE
Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), and Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA):
- You have the right to access, rectify, or purge off-chain profile data stored in Firestore.
- In-app sign-out ("Clear All Data & Cache") instantly wipes all local keys, databases, shared preferences, and cached profile artifacts from your device.
