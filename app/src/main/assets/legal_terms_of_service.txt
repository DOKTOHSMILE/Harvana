The protocol operates via deterministic cryptography: lost keys mean permanent loss of access.

4. SAVINGS GROUPS, COLLATERAL BONDS & GRACE PERIODS
When participating in Harvana Savings Circles (Rotating, Accumulative, or Fixed Goals):
- Contributions and bonds are locked in network escrow contracts governed by the smart contract rules.
- In rotating circles, collateral bonds guarantee performance.
- Defaults on round payments trigger the protocol grace period (up to 48 hours).
- If a default occurs, circle admins and members may vote on forgiveness or rehabilitation in accordance with on-chain consensus.
- Emergency grants and dissolution of stalled groups can be proposed and passed by member majority vote to guarantee funds are never permanently locked.

5. PEER-TO-PEER (P2P) ESCROW & CERTIFIED MEDIATION
In P2P fiat-to-crypto trades, Harvana acts solely as a technical escrow coordination layer. Certified moderators act as independent dispute arbiters who examine payment proofs. No warranty is made regarding counterparty fiat payment methods or banking reversals.
